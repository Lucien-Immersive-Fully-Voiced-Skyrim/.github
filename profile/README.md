## A Fully Voiced Companion with an Evolving Personality

Lucien Flavius begins his journey as a scholarly Imperial from Cyrodiil who arrives in Skyrim with limited combat experience and little confidence. Players can recruit him at Dead Man's Drink in Falkreath, where he is looking for a travelling companion. As the adventure progresses, his personality, confidence, and combat abilities develop through conversations, shared experiences, and dedicated training.

His dialogue system includes thousands of professionally recorded voice lines covering greetings, conversations, idle remarks, environmental observations, enemy encounters, and quest-related commentary. Lucien can react to locations, important events, and discoveries throughout Skyrim, helping make familiar journeys feel more personal. He also whispers while the player is sneaking, preserving immersion during stealth gameplay.

The character's development is not limited to combat. Lucien has his own interests, opinions, stories, and reactions, creating a companion who feels like an independent character rather than a conventional follower.

## Dynamic Training, Combat Styles, and Learnable Magic

Lucien features a dedicated progression system that allows him to develop his abilities naturally while travelling with the player. Every three in-game days, he improves skills according to the player's strengths and his currently selected combat style. He also develops independently, gradually improving the abilities associated with his preferred fighting approach.

Once sufficient trust has been established, players can unlock additional training conversations. These allow them to teach Lucien specific skills and accelerate his development through practical lessons.

His combat configuration supports several approaches, including warrior, mage, archer, and a default all-rounder style. Lucien can also learn additional spells by receiving the corresponding Spell Tomes, provided he has reached the required skill level. His available magic includes abilities from Skyrim's vanilla spell pool, with additional options when compatible content such as Apocalypse – Magic of Skyrim or Arcane Accessories is installed.

Players can manage his learned spells and combat preferences through dialogue and, when available, the Mod Configuration Menu (MCM). This makes it possible to adapt his abilities to different playstyles without sacrificing his individual character progression.

## Personal Quests, Exploration, and Interactive Storytelling

Lucien has dedicated personal quests that expand his background and provide additional adventures beyond the main Skyrim storyline. These quests become available as his relationship with the player develops, with conversations and shared experiences helping unlock new stages of his personal story.

His adventures include the Dwemer ruin Dumzbthar and the quest-related content surrounding The Oblivion Engine and Intruders. Completing his personal quest content also unlocks additional follower capabilities, including horse riding.

Beyond his own storyline, Lucien provides commentary on numerous Skyrim quests and locations. His dialogue covers major parts of the main quest, the College of Winterhold, the Companions, selected Daedric quests, Creation Club content, and supported quest mods.

He can also read selected books aloud when compatible books are available in his or the player's inventory. This adds another dimension to his scholarly personality and makes exploration, collecting books, and discovering Skyrim's lore more interactive.

## Custom Follower Interactions and Cross-Mod Conversations

One of Lucien's defining features is his ability to recognize and interact with characters introduced by other follower and NPC mods. These interactions range from simple recognition and commentary to complete conversations featuring specially written and recorded dialogue.

Some of his notable Level 3 interactions include:

- **Inigo** – Extensive custom conversations and companion banter.
- **Auri** – Dedicated dialogue scenes and interactions.
- **Hoth** – Specially recorded follower conversations.
- **Kaidan** – Custom interactions developed with contributions from Kaidan's voice and writing team.
- **Xelzaz** – Additional follower dialogue and interaction.
- **Lucifer the Argonian** – Dedicated conversations and character interactions.
- **Croc** – Additional dialogue scenes.
- **Anna NPCs** – Interactions with several characters from the Anna NPCs project.

These conversations are designed to make compatible followers feel like members of the same travelling party. Rather than treating other companions as unrelated NPCs, Lucien can acknowledge their presence and develop distinct conversational relationships with them.

Additional compatibility patches extend his commentary to supported quest mods, locations, items, and Creation Club content.

## Configuration, Compatibility, and Additional Content

Lucien uses his own follower management system rather than relying on Skyrim's standard follower framework. This allows him to maintain his individual progression, dialogue, and scripted interactions independently.

The official mod does not list mandatory additional requirements. However, SKSE64 and SkyUI enable his MCM functionality, providing access to additional configuration options, including combat preferences and spell management.

Because Lucien operates through a custom follower system, the author advises against using third-party follower management mods to control him. Frameworks such as Nether's Follower Framework (NFF), Amazing Follower Tweaks (AFT), and Extensible Follower Framework (EFF) may interfere with his scripted behaviour. Mods that assign followers to homes or otherwise override their behaviour can cause similar problems.

Additional patches are available for selected third-party mods and Creation Club content. The Anniversary Edition All-in-One Creation Club Patch combines supported Creation Club interactions into a single patch. It should not be installed alongside the corresponding individual Creation Club patches.
